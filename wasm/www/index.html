RickView WASM

Open a local .hdt or .hdt.zst file, or fetch one by URL. The file is parsed and queried entirely in your browser; nothing is uploaded.

Set the namespace to the prefix of the resource IRIs in the file, for example http://dbpedia.org/resource/ for DBpedia. Then browse the resources in the class tree, or open a resource directly by adding its suffix to the URL after #/.

Only HDT is supported in this build. OWL and RDF/XML input are server-only features.
